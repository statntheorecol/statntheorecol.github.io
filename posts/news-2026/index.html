2026 News

Some updates from the group for the academic year 2025-2026

  • Camille Schatz, who works with Frédéric and Christophe Barbraud on modelling albatross population dynamics, defended her PhD last fall. Bravo Camille! Now postdoc in the UK and finalizing an IPM for a declining population.

  • We hosted last December the annual meeting of the Theory and Modelling of Biodiversity research network in Bordeaux, together with Guilhem Sommeria-Klein (INRIA) and Mehdi Cherif (INRAE).

  • André, Didier Alard and Frédéric have had their manuscript on evaluating occupancy models with autocorrelation, inspired by Nouvelle-Aquitaine butterflies published in Journal of Agricultural, Biological and Environmental Statistics.

  • Christie has a recent preprint with Marcel Visser and Frédéric, on estimating density-dependence in great tit and lookalike simulated data.

  • The Journal of Statistical Ecology was launched this June! It was also introduced at the Ecostat meeting in Grenoble in 2026 by Frédéric.

Meetings of the year

  • Frédéric went in the fall 2025 to Umeå, Sweden for a gyrfalcon meeting, continuing by night train to Tromsø in Norway to work on rodent population dynamics reconstruction using camera trap data. With colleagues at UiT.
  • Frédéric and André went to the Cisstats meeting in Avignon (2-3 February 2026) on modelling heterogeous data, André presented our butterfly point process work.
  • Julien and Frédéric went to present their identifiability work at the Journées de Statistiques 2026 in Clermont-Ferrand, the first week of June.
