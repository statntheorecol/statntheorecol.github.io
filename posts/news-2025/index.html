2025 News

Some updates from the group in 2025

  • Julien Gibaud and Frédéric have a preprint on state-space model identifiability. We use spectral density to derive exhaustive summaries allowing to evaluate the intrinsic identifiability of state-space models.

  • André Luis Luza, Didier Alard and Frédéric have a preprint on occupancy models with autocorrelation, inspired by Nouvelle-Aquitaine butterflies. We have been also working on producing large-scale trends, now working on Integrated Species Distribution Models.

  • Frédéric went to Iceland in the spring to do ptarmigan fieldwork with long-term collaborator Olafur K. Nielsen. More on gyrfalcons and ptarmigan soon hopefully.

  • Christie Le Coeur went to visit collaborator Marcel Visser and his long-term great tit study site in the Netherlands. Christie has also been working on density-dependent integrated population models using simulated data.

Conferences of the year

  • Julien went to present his work at the Environmental and Ecological Statistics conference in Lancaster.
  • Christie and Frédéric went to the Evolutionary Demography annual conference at Pembroke College in Oxford.
